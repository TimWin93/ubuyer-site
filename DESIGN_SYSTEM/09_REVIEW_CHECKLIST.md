# Review Checklist

Use this after each screen or major implementation pass.

## Screen Identity

- Does this screen have one strong visual center?
- Is the screen memorable after 5 seconds?
- Is there a clear next action?

## Conversion Strength

- Is the main CTA obvious?
- Is anything stealing attention from the main CTA?
- Does the screen reduce hesitation or add it?

## Physical Quality

- Do primary elements feel layered and weighted?
- Do buttons feel pressable?
- Is there enough depth variation?
- Does motion support physicality?

## Visual Quality

- Does the screen avoid generic SaaS aesthetics?
- Is the typography intentional?
- Are color and materials coherent?
- Is there at least one strong design idea on the screen?

## AI Integration

- Does AI feel native to the funnel?
- Does AI guide, explain, or move the user forward?
- Does AI avoid feeling decorative?

## Mobile Quality

- Is the layout strong on a phone first?
- Is hierarchy obvious without zooming out?
- Does the screen still feel premium on smaller widths?

## Self-Critique

Before approving, answer:

1. What looks cheap?
2. What feels too safe?
3. What is missing physicality?
4. What does not create desire to click?
5. What 3 changes would give the biggest lift?
