# Brand Feel

## Brand Personality

The brand should feel:

- competent;
- calm;
- modern;
- expensive in craft, not flashy in tone;
- trustworthy under pressure.

## User Impression

The user should think:

- "Это быстро и понятно."
- "Это выглядит серьезно."
- "Мне действительно помогают."
- "Здесь не пытаются продавить, а ведут нормально."

## Tone of Voice

Voice principles:

- practical;
- warm;
- concise;
- grounded;
- confident.

Avoid:

- hype;
- startup jargon;
- exaggerated AI claims;
- vague marketing language.

## Product Identity

`AI LANDING V2` is a conversion product with an AI manager.

It should be framed as:

- estimator;
- explainer;
- guide;
- lead capture assistant.

Not as:

- “future of AI” concept page;
- heavy corporate service site;
- generic calculator widget.
