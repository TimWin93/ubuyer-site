# Color and Materials

## Color Direction

Base approach:

- background: warm light neutral;
- surfaces: cream / off-white / stone;
- text: graphite;
- accent: terracotta / warm orange;
- support accent: muted olive, mineral gray, or subtle sand.

## Material Cues

Primary surface feel:

- matte premium panel
- warm soft composite
- tactile product layer

Accent material cues:

- highlighted edge
- denser CTA surface
- premium input plate

## Color Usage Rules

- accent is for action and guidance;
- not every component should scream for attention;
- use light to define form, not pure saturation;
- support colors must stay grounded.

## Avoid

- purple AI palette;
- neon gradients;
- generic blue SaaS look;
- muddy low-contrast neutrals.
