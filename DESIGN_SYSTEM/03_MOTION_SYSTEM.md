# Motion System

## Motion Purpose

Motion exists to:

- clarify state;
- show causality;
- reinforce physicality;
- support conversion momentum.

## Timing

Recommended ranges:

- 120-180ms for press feedback;
- 180-280ms for hover/focus transitions;
- 240-420ms for reveals and state changes;
- 300-500ms for screen-level transitions.

## Motion Types

Use:

- subtle rise/fall;
- count-up for estimate values;
- slide + fade for AI reveals;
- smooth progress movement;
- soft stagger for grouped content.

## Motion Principles

- one clear cause, one clear effect;
- small distance, strong readability;
- ease should feel weighted, not robotic;
- repeated patterns should feel systemized.

## Motion Anti-Rules

Do not use:

- fade-in on everything;
- random floating animations;
- dramatic parallax;
- constant shimmer or glow;
- animation that competes with CTA.
