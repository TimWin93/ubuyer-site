# Creative Direction

## Core Direction

Project:
`AI LANDING V2`

Aesthetic direction:
`industrial premium tactile minimalism`

Supporting descriptors:

- warm
- physical
- guided
- precise
- conversion-focused

## Desired Emotional Effect

The interface should feel like:

- a serious product, not a promo page;
- an intelligent assistant, not a chatbot add-on;
- a premium estimator with mass, depth, and control;
- a fast system that helps the user act.

## The Big Idea

The site must feel like a working lead machine:

- the user enters data;
- the system reacts immediately;
- the AI manager explains and guides;
- the next step becomes obvious.

This is not a banner with a form.
This is a guided product funnel.

## Visual Center Rules

Each screen must have one dominant center:

- screen 1: interactive entry card;
- screen 2: estimate result card;
- screen 3: final lead capture card.

Everything else supports that center.

## What Makes It Memorable

- tactile pressable CTA buttons;
- cards that feel layered and weighted;
- AI that appears as part of the interface logic;
- strong mobile composition;
- warm material palette instead of generic AI visuals.

## What Must Never Happen

- generic SaaS hero with abstract gradient;
- weak visual hierarchy;
- random effects with no role;
- AI as a decorative floating widget;
- flat, weightless, default-looking frontend.
